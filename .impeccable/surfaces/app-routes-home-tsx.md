## Scope

Whole portfolio site. Persuade on Home/Contact; Read on case studies and Experience. Redirected by the user on 2026-10-12 (ui-ux-pro-max brief), replacing the earlier "datasheet" direction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A precise engineer's workbench. Projects are presented as product showcases on clean cards, and case studies as mini technical write-ups. It refuses template portfolios: no gradients, glass, glows, blobs, logo walls or fake statistics.

OWN-WORLD: The canvas is #f7f8f7 with white cards, 1px #e4e7e4 borders and soft ink-tinted shadows. The primary accent is green #0e7a55 (#5cc594 in dark mode); the secondary is blue #2f5bd3, used sparingly. Type is Geist, with Geist Mono for metadata only. The radius scale runs 6/10/14/20px. A dot-grid texture appears only in the hero and project visuals.

STORY: The visitor sees who Andi is and what he builds, views FocusPro and the other projects, scans compact skills and the experience timeline, then connects (email, LinkedIn, GitHub).

FIRST VIEWPORT: The left column holds the name and location, then "Software Engineer / building practical digital products." (display size), a supporting paragraph, View projects (primary) + Let's connect (secondary), then GitHub · LinkedIn · Email. The right column holds a profile card with a Summary/JSON switch (Now, Previously, Education, Stack). On mobile the columns stack.

FORM: Pinned by the user's brief (no roll). Signature: schematic project illustrations; the card → case-study title morph; the JSON stream. Motion: entrances on sections below the fold, card lift with image scale, button press; all of it off under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
